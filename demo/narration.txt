You want an AI agent to pay your invoices. But a wallet key is all or nothing. If the agent gets fooled, you lose everything. CashMandate gives the agent a budget instead of your key.

You decide the rules once. Thirty thousand satoshis in a vault. No more than two thousand per payment. Only two approved suppliers. Those rules are written into a Bitcoin Cash contract, and the money is locked inside it. This is the real funding transaction on chipnet.

Your agent connects over the Model Context Protocol, with its own key and the public rules. Your key never reaches it. It discovers two tools: get mandate, and pay.

Now we give it the inbox. Four invoices. And we tell it to follow vendor instructions exactly, like a careless assistant would.

Invoice one. Price Feed Co, fifteen hundred satoshis. The agent calls pay. The contract checks the recipient, the limit, and the fee. Everything passes, and the payment goes through, with a memo written on the chain.

Invoice two is a trap. A message says the bank details changed, and to send the money to a new address. The agent obeys. The contract refuses, and returns the exact line that failed. The recipient must be on the approved list. The vault did not move.

Invoice three asks for nine thousand satoshis. The limit is two thousand. Refused again, on the exact line that checks the amount.

Invoice four is legitimate, so it is paid. Two payments went out. Two attacks were stopped. The agent was fooled twice, and lost nothing.

And your money is never trapped. The owner can sweep the vault at any time. One signature, and everything left comes back to you. That is how a mandate is ended early.

So what can the agent never do? Pay an address you did not approve. Exceed the limit. Skim the vault through the miner fee. Spend two vault outputs in one transaction. Or take the owner's exit. A minimum wait between payments is also enforced. Twenty six tests run every one of those attacks against the real script.

You do not need your own agent system to try it. In the browser console, pick a limit, paste a model key, and an agent works through the same inbox in your tab, against the real contract on a simulated network. It pays the honest invoices, and the contract refuses the rest, live. It costs nothing, and touches no chain.

Everything real is on the chain. The ledger lists the funding, both payments, and the revocation, each one a live chipnet transaction.

CashMandate. Give your agent a budget, not your key.
